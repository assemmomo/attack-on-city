ATTACK ON CITY
Monsters pour over the wall from every side. Fight with sword, bow and cables — and keep the captive tied up in the plaza alive. If she falls, the village is lost.

THE SIEGE

  • Protect the captive — 30% of the horde goes for her
  • Monsters leap the wall and climb buildings
  • The longer you last, the bigger the horde
  • A giant boss leaps the wall every 5 waves
  • F on a monster: cable kick and knockdown
  • Fast sword combos · one-arrow kills
  • Ground slam · dual-cable traversal
Press F to fire both cables. Aim at a monster to fly in and kick it down; press F again to release.

Movement guide

CONTROL THE MOMENTUM

W A S D Move
MOUSE Look / aim
V Toggle first / third person
1 / 2 Switch weapon: 1 = sword, 2 = bow
SHIFT Sprint
SPACE Jump / wall leap
C / CTRL Slide (only at full sprint speed, 1.5 s cooldown, press again each time)
W + SPACE Fast-climb a building (face it, hold both)
F Fire / release both cables — aim at a monster and press F for a cable kick
X Super strike (one charge every 25 kills): slam the ground in front of you — every monster inside the long red/blue rectangle dies
LEFT MOUSE (SWORD) Attack: sword slash, second slash, third slash
LEFT MOUSE (SWORD, IN AIR) Ground slam: raise the sword with both hands, then dive onto the spot your crosshair points at (a red circle shows it); the fall height sets the damage to every monster inside the shockwave circle
SPACE, THEN LEFT MOUSE (SWORD) Leap strike: jump from the ground and attack — somersault and slam; every monster in the small circle is hurt, thrown 3 m into the air, falls sprawled on the ground and gets up again if it survives
Q Block: guard for half a second — monster hits do no damage, but you cannot move or do anything else meanwhile
HOLD LEFT MOUSE 1 s (SWORD) Charged lunge: pull the sword back while a ring fills around the crosshair (the screen shakes slightly when full), keep holding as long as you like, then release to dash forward with a heavy sweeping cut — every monster in the path is hurt and thrown (a short click is still the normal slash)
LEFT MOUSE (BOW) Hold to aim and draw; release to shoot (works while flying)
RIGHT MOUSE Cancel bow aiming without shooting
SPACE (AIR) Cut cables and drop
ESC Pause / release mouse
DEFAULT KEYS SHOWN · YOU CAN CHANGE THEM IN SETTINGS → CONTROLS
Press V to switch between first-person and third-person camera views at any time during play. The F key launches both cables toward the crosshair. They can attach to any world surface, including the ground and surfaces at any elevation; the sky cannot be targeted. Press F again to release. Press Space while airborne to cut both cables and fall. Cables reach at most 95 m. Wall-run along any wall (any direction) by jumping up beside it first (never from the ground); face a building and hold W + Space to climb it without a cable.
